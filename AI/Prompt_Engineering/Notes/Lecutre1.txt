
:: Prompt Engineering :

- It is a relatively new discipline for developing and optimizing prompts to efficiently use language models(LMs) for a wide variety of applications and research topics.
- Prompt engineering skills help to better understand the capabilities and limitations of large language models(LLMs).


:: LLMs( Large Language Models ) :

- LLMs are AI models trained on massive text data.
- They learn to predict and generate text.
- Ex : GPT-4 , BERT , LLaMA , claude , Gemini , Deepseek.


:--> What Makes an LLM "Large" ?

- "Large" : millions to billions of parameters.
- The more parameters , the better it understand language.
- Trained on terabytes of text data.





:--> Capabilities of LLMs :-

1. Answer questions.
2. Summarize text.
3. Translate languages.
4. Generate stories or code.
5. Chat like a human.
6. Write emails , blogs , reports.

:: Prompt Design Strategies :



1. Clarity and Specificity :-  [ Clear task + specific requirements + expected output format ]
- Be clear and specific in your prompts. Vague requests can lead to equally vague responses.

- Ex :

:--> ❌ Bad prompt:
"Analyze this sales data."

->AI ko pata nahi hai ki kya analyze karna hai.



:--> ✅ Good prompt:
"Analyze the sales data and calculate total sales, average sales, highest-selling product, and monthly sales trend. Present the results in a table."








2. Providing Context :-  [ Context → Better understanding → Better analysis ]
- Matlab AI ko background/information do taaki woh situation ko properly understand kar sake.
- Ex :


:--> ❌ Without context:

"Why did sales decrease?"
->AI ko pata hi nahi hai ki business kya hai, kaunsa period hai, etc.

:--> ✅ With context:

"You are analyzing sales data for an e-commerce company. Sales decreased by 15% in March compared with February. The dataset contains product, region, revenue, and order count. Identify possible reasons for the decline and suggest 3 business actions."

->Ab AI ko business + data + problem ka context mil gaya.











3. Use of Instructive language :-  [ Calculate → Identify → Explain ]
- Matlab prompt mein direct commands/instructions use karo. AI ko clearly batao ki kya karna hai.
- Ex :

:--> ❌ Weak:

"Sales data ke baare mein kuch batao."

:--> ✅ Instructive:

"Calculate the month-over-month sales growth, identify the month with the highest growth, and provide two possible business reasons for the result."

:: Types of Prompt Engineering :


1. Zero-Shot Prompting :

- Zero shot prompts require the model to respond to a task without any prior examples. It's like asking someone to perform a task they've never done before , relying solely on their existing knowledge.

- Ex :  Asking GPT-4o " What is the capital of France ?" expects an accurate response based on its pre-training , without needing examples of capital cities.



2. Few-Shot Prompting :

- Few shot prompts give the model a few examples to "learn" from before asking it to complete a similar task. This is like teaching someone through a handful of examples and then expecting them to apply that knowledge to a new situation.

- Ex : Providing GPT-3 with a list of country-capital pairs before asking , "What is the capital of Japan ?" helps guide the model towards the correct answer type.





3. Chain-of-Thought Prompting : [ Complex problem ko logical steps mai solve karwana. ]

- chain-of-thought prompting involves asking the model to detail its thought process , leading to more complex problem-solving. It encourages the model to "think aloud" , making it easier to follow its logic.

- Ex : "Analyze the monthly sales data step-by-step. First identify missing values, then calculate total and average sales, identify the highest-sales month, and finally give a business insight."

:-> Structure :-  [ Instruction ] + [ Context + Role , advance / Output Format ] + [ Constraints ]

:-> Ex :-

"Clean this data [Instruction] using Python [Context] and remove all rows with missing values [ constraints ]."
