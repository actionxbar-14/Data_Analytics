## STEPS :


1. Build a star Schema . [ a fact in the middle , dimensions around it ] 
2. Understand before you change. [ always read the grain first ] 
3. Protect the numbers [ Facts ]. [ know your totals , re-check after every change ]
4. Every column earns its place. [ If it does not help the report , drop it ]
